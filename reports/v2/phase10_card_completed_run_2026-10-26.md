## Interpretation and limitations

- XGBoost has strong time-split performance in this simulator: 99% event recall and 99% transaction recall at approximately 1% legitimate FPR.
- The combined lane does not outperform XGBoost on every held-out pattern; notably, its SLOW_DRIP event recall is 64% versus XGBoost's 82%.
- Precision is evaluated at an assumed 0.6% fraud prevalence. It should not be interpreted as a measured production precision.
- The simulator may contain shared signals across fraud patterns. Very high leave-one-pattern-out scores do not yet prove robust detection of genuinely novel real-world fraud.
- Validate on an appropriately licensed real transaction dataset before making real-world performance claims.
