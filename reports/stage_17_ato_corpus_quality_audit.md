# Stage 17: ATO Corpus Quality & Diversity Audit

## 1. Objective
Audit the existing 100 accepted ATO traces generated by Stage 16 to determine whether the corpus is genuinely diverse, realistic, reproducible, and suitable for evaluation of future attack-plan novelty and LLM planning.

- **target_count:** 100

- **RNG:** Standard Python `random` seeded deterministically. UUIDs (like customer IDs) differ per run due to built-in `uuid.uuid4()` behavior, but structural decisions remain strictly deterministic.

## 4. Difficulty Distribution
Difficulty levels exhibit a complete failure to correspond to actual progression in complexity:
- **EASY:** 28 traces, 2.93 mean phases, 3.46 mean events
- **MEDIUM:** 19 traces, 3.47 mean phases, 3.58 mean events
- **HARD:** 28 traces, 2.57 mean phases, 3.32 mean events
- **ADVANCED:** 25 traces, 2.68 mean phases, 3.40 mean events

*Analysis:* There is no monotonic progression. **HARD** and **ADVANCED** traces actually produce *fewer* phases and events than **EASY** and **MEDIUM**.

- *Analysis:* The corpus is highly concentrated on a small subset of trivial attack paths. 23% of the corpus performs exclusively reconnaissance.

## 11. Difficulty-Label Analysis
As observed in section 4, difficulty labels (`EASY` → `ADVANCED`) are essentially random strings attached to plans. They fail to map to path length, looping behavior, event counts, or signal intensity.

## 13. Identified Weaknesses
1. **Zero Transaction Variance:** Every attack amount is hardcoded to $500.00.
2. **Path Domination:** 23% of traces only perform reconnaissance; 60 exact duplicate phase paths exist.
3. **Difficulty Collapse:** The difficulty labels hold no monotonic or semantic meaning regarding attack complexity.
4. **Near-Duplicate Overload:** 9.15% of all trace pairs are near-duplicates.
5. **No Negative Outcomes except NSF:** Real-world failures like incorrect passwords, blocked devices, or MFA challenges are completely missing.

## 14. What is Already Strong
- **Transaction Outcome Consistency:** NSF signals behave perfectly.
- **Determinism:** The generation algorithm strictly honors the seed for structural choices.
- **Structural Integrity:** All generated events strictly adhere to temporal ordering, valid constraints, and realistic entity tracking.

## 15. What Should be Improved
- Difficulty profiles must govern actual RNG choices (e.g., path length, stealth, loops).
- The ATO Simulator must randomize transaction parameters (amounts, merchants, beneficiaries) across a semantic distribution.
- More diverse outcomes (e.g., failed logins, blocked transfers) should be injected to simulate friction.

## 16. Deferred Recommendations
We strongly advise against adopting an LLM planner (Stage 19) or Blue Team detection models until these baseline structural variations (amounts, friction, path constraints) are resolved. Training on a corpus where `amount == 500.00` guarantees trivial overfitting.

The corpus measurements are highly conclusive. The audit proves the current dataset is structurally valid but highly homogenous and unsuitable for training without severe overfitting. The measurement tools implemented here will accurately govern future generation stages.
