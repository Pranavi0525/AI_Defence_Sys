# BLUE TEAM INTEGRATION SPECIFICATION

## 1. Overview & Event Flow Diagrams
This document specifies how the Blue Team (detection and modeling) should consume the synthetic attack corpora generated by the Red Team. The Red Team simulator is now **FROZEN** and outputs a deterministic, stateful, physically consistent dataset of `ACCOUNT_TAKEOVER` (ATO) and `AUTHORIZED_PUSH_PAYMENT` (APP) attack paths.
The final persisted datasets delivered alongside this handoff contain exactly **97 ATO** traces and **156 APP** traces.

### 1.1 Confirmed Observable Event Flow
Attacks generate sequential observable events. The confirmed observable event types present in **BOTH** ATO and APP corpora include:
- `SESSION_LOGIN`
- `BENEFICIARY_ADDITION`
- `TRANSACTION`

*(Note: `DEVICE_REGISTRATION` is also present, but it is structurally exclusive to the ATO corpus).*

### 2.2 The Observable Trace (Features)
**DO USE THIS FOR FEATURE ENGINEERING AND MODEL TRAINING.**
*   **Isolation Guarantee:** The `observable_trace` is structurally isolated by its schema and was explicitly verified in this artifact run to contain **ZERO** leakage of ground-truth labels. It does not contain the `attack_family`, the `difficulty`, or internal simulator states.
*   **Balance Integrity:** Account balances are intentionally stripped from observable transaction events. As verified during simulator generation, internal balances strictly adhere to ledger invariants, but the Blue Team must reconstruct these balances using historical customer baselines, exactly as they would in production.

## 3. Ground Truth & Evaluation Labels
**DO USE THIS ONLY FOR LABELING, STRATIFICATION, AND EVALUATION.**

**Every persisted record in `reports/ato_corpus_raw.json` and `reports/app_corpus_raw.json` is an attack trace by construction â€” there is no separate `is_fraud` field in `AttackGroundTruth`.**
*   **Ingest Labeling:** The Blue Team should apply `fraud=1` to every record from these two files at ingest time. 
*   **Legitimate Population (Normal World):** A `fraud=0` (legitimate) population must come from a separate Normal World / legitimate-event source â€” these corpora alone cannot teach a detector what legitimate activity looks like.
*   **Target Amount:** There is no `target_amount` scalar within the ground truth schema. If an intended-extraction amount is needed, it must be inferred from the observable transaction sequence itself, not read from ground_truth.

## 4. Feature Engineering Suggestions
Because `BENEFICIARY_ADDITION` is a confirmed observable event type present in both attack families, it is a highly viable signal. The Blue Team should strongly consider deriving temporal and sequence features based on its relationship to subsequent transactions. 

Candidate features include:
*   `beneficiary_added_before_transaction` (Boolean: Was a new payee established in the same session?)
*   `time_from_beneficiary_add_to_transaction` (Time delta: Critical for distinguishing the slow, hesitant coercion in APP attacks from the fast, automated extraction in ATO attacks).

## 5. Attack Families & Difficulty Semantics

### 5.1 Account Takeover (ATO)
Represents unauthorized access by a third party.
*   **Signatures:** `DEVICE_REGISTRATION` immediately followed by `TRANSACTION` or `BENEFICIARY_ADDITION`. High transaction fragmentation (many splits). No hesitation gaps.
*   **Difficulty:** Easy = fast/loud. Advanced = Low-and-slow, multi-phase infiltration over days.

### 5.2 Authorized Push Payment (APP)
Represents a legitimate customer being socially engineered.
*   **Signatures:** Uses known, trusted devices and existing sessions. Heavy hesitation gaps (10-120 minutes) between `BENEFICIARY_ADDITION` and payment execution.
*   **Difficulty:** Easy = one-shot fast transfer. Advanced = Heavy friction, large gaps, and multiple failed retries due to bank limits before successful extraction.

## 6. Known Limitations
1.  **APP 'EASY' Diversity:** A basic, one-shot APP scam lacks structural complexity. The Red Team generator correctly refuses to manufacture fake diversity, meaning you will receive fewer mathematically unique `EASY` APP traces compared to other buckets. This reflects reality.
2.  **No Ledger Handoff:** The full physical `WorldState` ledger is intentionally discarded after simulation. The Blue Team must construct entity graphs and historical baselines strictly from the provided `ObservableAttackTrace` events and the Normal World baseline logs.

## 8. Novelty Validation — What It Means for Blue Team
- The Red Team's Novelty Engine is a generation-quality mechanism that strictly rejects structurally duplicate attacks, but it is scoped by **family + difficulty**.
- **Verified Finding:** Across the persisted corpora, there are precisely **6 ATO** and **3 APP** duplicate structural fingerprints. ALL of these occur *across* different difficulty buckets; ZERO duplicates exist within the same (family, difficulty) bucket.
- **WARNING:** The Blue Team should NOT assume every trace has a globally unique behavioral fingerprint, and should NOT attempt to reuse the Red Team's novelty/realism validators as a fraud-detection mechanism. Those are quality gates for generation, not detectors.

## 9. Train/Test Splitting Guidance
- The unit of splitting MUST be the AttackRecord (the observable_trace + ground_truth pair). Never split or shuffle individual events within a trace.
- Do not shuffle the observable_trace and ground_truth structures independently from one another.
- If multiple traces share a customer_id, you must consider splitting by customer/entity. Failing to do so will result in the same customer's baseline appearing in both train and test partitions, risking data leakage.
- *Suggestion:* An 80/20 AttackRecord-level split is a standard starting point for baseline modeling.

## 10. Recommended Metrics
- **Standard:** Precision, Recall, F1, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, Confusion Matrix.
- **Family-Stratified:** You must report ATO metrics and APP metrics SEPARATELY, not just pooled. The two families have structurally distinct signatures (see Section 5), and a pooled metric can easily mask a detector that only succeeds on one family while completely failing the other.
- **Difficulty-Stratified:** Report recall broken out by easy/medium/hard/advanced for each family. Frame your evaluation by asking: *"Does the detector's performance degrade as attack difficulty increases?"* That is the specific hypothesis the difficulty tiering exists to test.
- **Sample Size Warning:** The APP-easy and ATO-easy sample sizes are physically constrained (6 and 22 traces respectively). Any difficulty-stratified metrics on these specific buckets will possess wide uncertainty bounds compared to the 25/50-sized buckets. Do not over-interpret a metric computed on 6 examples.
